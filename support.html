I bought the Career unlock on another device

Open Agency → Settings → Restore purchase, or tap Restore purchase on the season-two screen.

Can I move my career to a new iPhone?

Turn on Settings → Back up to iCloud on the old device, then on the new one open Settings and tap Restore iCloud copy.

Why did my client leave?

Clients leave when they’re Unhappy or Furious for a while — usually from not playing, feeling ignored, or a broken promise. Rival agencies court unhappy players. Meet them early.
