Privacy policy

Last updated: October 12, 2026

The short version

We don’t know who you are. The game has no accounts, no ads and no tracking. Your career is saved on your device.

What stays on your device

  • Your career save file. If you turn on Back up to iCloud, a copy is stored in your own iCloud account, which we can’t access.
  • Your settings.

Anonymous usage data

If you leave Share anonymous usage data on, the app sends anonymous events (for example “a season ended” or “a deal was done”) to TelemetryDeck so we can improve the game. Anonymous error and performance events are included in this usage data. These events are not linked to you, contain no names and no save data, and are not used for tracking or advertising. You can turn this off in Settings at any time.

Purchases

Purchases are handled by Apple. To confirm what you own, the app shares your purchase receipts and a random, anonymous app ID with RevenueCat, our purchase-processing service. We receive confirmation of what was bought, never your name or payment details. RevenueCat’s privacy policy: revenuecat.com/privacy.

Game Center

If you sign in to Game Center, achievements and leaderboard scores are shared with Apple’s Game Center under your Game Center profile, according to Apple’s privacy policy.

Children

The game doesn’t knowingly collect personal information from anyone.
